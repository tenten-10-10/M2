# SaaS Metrics Cheat Sheet

Every key formula and a healthy benchmark, on one page. Educational only — not
financial advice; benchmarks vary by stage and segment.

## Unit economics
- **LTV** = (ARPA × Gross margin %) ÷ Monthly churn %
- **CAC** = Sales & marketing spend ÷ New customers
- **LTV:CAC** = LTV ÷ CAC — *healthy: 3–5 : 1*
- **CAC payback** = CAC ÷ (ARPA × Gross margin %) — *healthy: < 12 months*

## Growth
- **MRR (ending)** = Starting + New + Expansion − Contraction − Churned
- **Net new MRR** = New + Expansion − Contraction − Churned
- **ARR** = MRR × 12
- **ARPU/ARPA** = Total MRR ÷ Customers
- **ACV** = (Total contract value − one-time fees) ÷ term (years)
- **Trial conversion** = Paid conversions ÷ Trials × 100 — *opt-in trial ~15–25%*

## Retention
- **Customer (logo) churn** = Customers lost ÷ Customers at start — *healthy: 1–3%/mo (SMB)*
- **Annualised churn** ≈ 1 − (1 − monthly)¹²
- **NRR** = (Start + Expansion − Contraction − Churn) ÷ Start × 100 — *good: >100%, best: 110%+*
- **GRR** = (Start − Contraction − Churn) ÷ Start × 100 — *best: >90%*
- **DAU/MAU (stickiness)** = DAU ÷ MAU × 100 — *good: 20%+, daily-habit: 50%+*

## Efficiency
- **Rule of 40** = Revenue growth % + Profit margin % — *pass: ≥ 40%*
- **Magic number** = (Current Q rev − Prior Q rev) × 4 ÷ Prior Q S&M — *efficient: 0.75–1.0+*
- **Quick ratio** = (New + Expansion) ÷ (Churned + Contraction) — *best: ≥ 4*
- **Gross margin** = (Revenue − Cost of revenue) ÷ Revenue × 100 — *SaaS: 70–85%*
- **ARR per employee** = ARR ÷ FTEs — *healthy: $150k–$250k+*
- **ARR multiple** = ARR × multiple — *illustrative only; set by the market*

## Cash
- **Gross burn** = Monthly cash out
- **Net burn** = Cash out − Cash in
- **Burn multiple** = Net burn ÷ Net new ARR — *amazing: < 1.0*
- **Runway** = Cash on hand ÷ Net monthly burn — *comfortable: 18+ months*
